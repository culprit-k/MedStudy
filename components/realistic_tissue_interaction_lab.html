Learning objective
Understand tool positioning and spatial relationships. The model gives visual feedback only; it does not assess safe technique.

Prototype / safety notice: This is a procedural graphics demonstration, not clinically realistic tissue simulation. The layers, incision, probe response and stitches are visual approximations; there is no validated anatomy, calibrated tissue properties, true cutting or tearing mechanics, bleeding physiology, haptic feedback, sterilisation workflow, or clinical scoring. Do not use it to learn or perform an operation without qualified supervision.
