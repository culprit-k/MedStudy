Prototype / safety notice: This is a procedural graphics demonstration, not clinically realistic tissue simulation. The layers, incision, probe response and stitches are visual approximations; there is no validated anatomy, tissue-force model, bleeding physiology, haptic feedback, sterilisation workflow, or clinical scoring. Do not use it to learn or perform an operation without qualified supervision.
